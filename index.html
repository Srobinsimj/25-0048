About the Liberia First Division

The Liberia First Division League (LFDL) is the top tier of association football in the Republic of Liberia, organized and sanctioned by the Liberia Football Association (LFA) under the auspices of the Confederation of African Football (CAF) and FIFA.

The Liberia First Division League stands as the most prestigious football competition in the nation, bringing together the finest clubs, players, and coaches from every corner of the country.

— Milestone separator marking a new chapter in Liberian football history —

Every season, sixteen elite clubs compete in a round-robin format, battling fiercely across months of matches for the coveted First Division championship title, continental qualification, and eternal football glory.


Founded with the vision of elevating Liberian football to continental and global prominence, the league has produced numerous international players who have gone on to represent the national team with distinction.

Championship Honours & Records

League Timeline

1976

Liberia First Division established under the Liberia Football Association.

1989

League suspended due to civil conflict; football becomes a symbol of peace.

2003

League reinstated following the end of armed conflict and national reconciliation.

2018

President George Weah launches major investment into football infrastructure nationwide.

2024

Record season attendance and expanded 16-team format introduced across the division.

League Regulations

All participating clubs must maintain a minimum squad size of twenty registered players throughout the entire competitive season without exception.

— Article 12: Squad Requirements, LFA Competition Rules 2024 —

Clubs found guilty of fielding ineligible players shall be deducted three points per offence and may face further disciplinary hearings before the committee.

— Article 27: Disciplinary Procedures, LFA Competition Rules 2024 —

The bottom two clubs at the end of the season shall be automatically relegated to the Second Division with absolutely no play-off reprieve available.

— Article 33: Promotion & Relegation, LFA Competition Rules 2024 —
